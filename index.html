Jozi Youth Dance Company Precision Meets Passion

Founded by Jayd Swart, developing South Africa’s finest young contemporary and theatrical dance talent through world-class choreography and rigorous artistic discipline.

Explore Productions Book Audition / Class

A stage for every young dancer in Johannesburg

Jayd Swart founded Jozi Youth Dance Company on a simple conviction: talent is everywhere in this city, and it deserves a professional stage. The company brings together diverse young dancers from across Johannesburg and trains them to perform at the standard of a working company.

Every season, dancers take part in groundbreaking productions, from contemporary adaptations to showcase pieces, built with original choreography, full staging and live audiences. They learn technique, stagecraft and the discipline that carries a dancer through a career.

Jayd Swart, Founder & Artistic Director

Dance programs & training divisions

Contemporary & Expressive Performance

Floor work, release technique and improvisation, shaped into performance pieces that tell a story with the whole body.

Cecchetti & Classical Ballet Technique

Structured Cecchetti training for line, balance and strength, building the technical base every other style depends on.

Youth Choreography & Stage Ensemble

Dancers create and rehearse original work, then perform it as part of the company ensemble in full productions.
